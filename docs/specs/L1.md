# L1 - High-Level Requirements

Source of truth: the HTML mocks in `docs/mocks/` (with `docs/mocks/manifest.json` for routes and states). Where a mock and this document disagree, the mock wins and this document is corrected.

## L1-001: Invite-only authentication
The system shall allow only invited people to sign in, shall let them recover a forgotten password, and shall let them sign out.

## L1-002: Research questions dashboard (Field home)
The system shall present a home page that summarises the catalogue and shows the status and answer of each of the six core research questions, the most impactful problems and the latest research runs.

## L1-003: Organization management
The system shall let users browse, filter, sort, search, view, create, edit, archive, restore, delete, tag, import and export organizations that serve Christian youth.

## L1-004: People management
The system shall let users browse, view, create, edit and delete the key people of organizations, including their role, organization and optional LinkedIn link.

## L1-006: Problem management and ranking
The system shall let users create, view, edit, delete and rank the problems that organizations target by impact (reach × severity × tractability) and link problems to organizations.

## L1-007: Web research runs
The system shall let users start web research runs that find organizations, and people, shall show run progress, and shall let users stop a run.

## L1-008: Finding review
The system shall hold every research result as a finding that a person accepts, edits or rejects before it changes a record, with sources, confidence and undo.

## L1-009: Core questions answers
The system shall answer the six core questions from the catalogue, show sources and completeness per answer, and let users re-run research for any question.

## L1-010: Map
The system shall show organizations on a world map, filterable, and shall call out organizations without a single location and geographic gaps.

## L1-011: Global search and command palette
The system shall let users find organizations, people, problems and findings from one search page and from a keyboard-driven command palette with shortcuts.

## L1-012: Notifications
The system shall notify users of research and team events, let them read and mark notifications read, and show transient and persistent in-app messages.

## L1-013: Settings and preferences
The system shall let each user manage their profile, research defaults, trusted sources, appearance and notification preferences.

## L1-014: Resilience and error handling
The system shall handle offline use, failed requests, missing pages, forbidden access, server errors and unsupported browsers with clear recovery paths and shall protect users from losing unsaved work.

## L1-015: Responsive, themed and keyboard-accessible UI
The system shall work at the viewport sizes in the mocks, in light and dark themes, with keyboard operation.
