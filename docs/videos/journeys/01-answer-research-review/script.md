# 01 · Answer, research, review

## Welcome

Harvest Map exists to answer six questions about the organizations that serve Christian youth. Who is helping young people with AI, loneliness and the search for meaning? Who is chasing moonshot problems, who is working on the most impactful one, and who are the people behind the work?

This video walks through the three journeys that answer those questions. You read the answers, you run research where they are thin, and you review what the research finds.

## Answer the questions

Start on the Questions page. The header counts the six questions, and how many are answered or only partial. Each question shows its number, its status, and how many web sources stand behind it.

Answers are links to real records. Question three ranks problems by impact score, which is reach times severity times tractability, and names the organization leading each one. Scripture in every language by 2033 tops the list at ninety-four, led by illumiNations.

Each answer loads on its own. If one fails, only that card says it couldn't load the answer and offers Retry. The other five keep working.

## Run research

When an answer is thin, select Re-run on that question, or press R anywhere. The Ask the web dialog opens with that question already chosen.

Pick what to look for: organisations, people, and LinkedIn profiles. Then pick a depth. Quick takes about a minute, Standard about five, and Deep about twenty, with more sources. The default depth comes from your settings.

If you choose Something else, you have to write the question. Leave it empty, and Run stops with Fix one thing to save.

Select Run and the run is queued. Behind the scenes, Claude searches the web and returns each result as a finding, with its sources and a confidence score.

Progress shows on the Research page. Every run is listed with who started it, how many findings it produced, and whether it is done or failed.

## Review a finding

Nothing the web says changes a record on its own. Every result waits in Findings to review. Each card shows the statement, who it is about, the source, and the confidence. When confidence is low, the reason sits right there, for example, company claim, no independent source.

Select Accept and the card updates at once. The person or organization is created or updated, an activity entry records who accepted it and from which run, and you have thirty seconds to undo. Reject keeps the source, and changes nothing.

To adjust a finding first, select Edit. The Review finding dialog shows the record it updates, the statement with its confidence, source count and status, the fields it will write, and every source.

A required field left empty, such as the role, stops Accept with Enter a role. Your other edits are kept.

## The loop closes

Go back to the Questions page and the answer has moved. Kara Powell, accepted a moment ago, now leads the key people, and her sources count toward the answer.

That is the whole loop. Read the answers, ask the web where they are thin, and decide what the web found. Next, we browse and edit the organizations behind these answers.
