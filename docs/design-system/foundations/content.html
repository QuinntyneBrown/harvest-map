Content

Minimal text. Nouns for labels, verbs for buttons, numbers carry the story.

Voice

  • Calm, specific, brief.
  • Sentence case everywhere except product and organisation names.
  • Gentle brand moments in titles only: The field is ripe., Nothing grows here.

Numbers and dates

  • 1,284 with separators; units after (140,000 leaders).
  • Relative in lists (Yesterday), absolute on details (Tue 14 Oct).
  • Subtitles: counts joined by middots: 14 tracked · 9 verified · 5 unverified.

Error formula

What happened + what to do + action. Couldn't load organisations. Try again, or share reference HM-4F2A.

Empty formula

Title (state) + one sentence (why/what) + one action. No organisations yet · Add one or let research find them · Ask the web
