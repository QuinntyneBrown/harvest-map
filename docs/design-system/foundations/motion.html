Foundation

Motion

Micro-animations that explain: rise, lift, press, fill, pulse. Chrome-native: @starting-style, scroll-driven, view transitions, linear() springs.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v1.0
  • Overview
  • Durations
  • Easing
  • Choreography
  • Reduced motion
  • Tokens

Overview

  • Rise: lists enter 40ms apart with @starting-style.
  • Reveal: lower sections fade up with animation-timeline: view().
  • Lift: cards rise 2px on hover with a linear() spring.
  • Press: buttons and chips scale down.
  • Fill: score rings animate a registered @property.
  • Morph: page titles carry view-transition-name across pages.
  • Ambient: the WebGPU field drifts.

Durations

Easing

Choreography

  • Hover 120ms, reveal 200ms, dialogs 320ms, score fill 500ms.
  • Loops (spinner, pulse, ripple) have their own tokens.
  • No animation over 500ms except loops and the ambient field.

Reduced motion

Under prefers-reduced-motion every duration token becomes 0.01ms, --stagger becomes 0, scroll reveals and pin ripples are removed and the WebGPU field draws one still frame.
